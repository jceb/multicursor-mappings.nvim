1. INTRODUCTION                                   *multicursor-mappings-intro*

multicursor-mappings provides convenient key mappings and helper functions
built on top of Neovim's native multicursor engine.

For details on the underlying built-in multicursor implementation, see
|multicursor|.

==============================================================================
2. REQUIREMENTS                           *multicursor-mappings-requirements*

- Neovim 0.13.0 or later with built-in multicursor support.

==============================================================================
3. KEY MAPPINGS                               *multicursor-mappings-mappings*

Below are the default key mappings provided by the plugin:

Normal Mode Mappings ~

<Esc>                                           *<Esc>* *multicursor-<Esc>*
<A-i>                                           *<A-i>* *multicursor-<A-i>*
                Clear all multicursors in the current buffer.

<A-j>                                           *<A-j>* *multicursor-<A-j>*
                Place a cursor at the current position and move down.
                Accepts a |[count]|.

<A-k>                                           *<A-k>* *multicursor-<A-k>*
                Place a cursor at the current position and move up.
                Accepts a |[count]|.

<A-=>                                           *<A-=>* *multicursor-<A-=>*
                Toggle multicursor follow mode (delegates to `q=`).

<A-m>                                           *<A-m>* *multicursor-<A-m>*
                Move back to the previous cursor and delete it (delegates to
                `[CQ`).

Normal and Visual Mode Mappings ~

<A-a>                                           *<A-a>* *multicursor-<A-a>*
                Normal mode: Place cursors at all occurrences of the word
                under the cursor.
                Visual mode: Place cursors at all occurrences of the current
                selection.

<A-n>                                           *<A-n>* *multicursor-<A-n>*
                Normal mode: Place a cursor and jump to the next occurrence
                of the word under the cursor.
                Visual mode: Place a cursor and jump to the next occurrence
                of the current selection.

<A-S-n>                                     *<A-S-n>* *multicursor-<A-S-n>*
                Normal mode: Place a cursor and jump backward to the
                previous occurrence of the word under the cursor.
                Visual mode: Place a cursor and jump backward to the
                previous occurrence of the current selection.

<A-C-n>                                     *<A-C-n>* *multicursor-<A-C-n>*
                Place a cursor and advance search to the next match.
                Accepts a |[count]|.
